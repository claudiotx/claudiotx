## About Me

I build **agentic applications**, orchestrate **AI agent fleets**, and scale the software and teams behind them.

Over **15+ years**, I’ve delivered **70+ applications** across industries and scaled engineering teams to **30+ developers**. My work combines hands-on engineering, system architecture, and technical leadership—from early-stage startup products to distributed enterprise systems.

My focus is **Software 3.0: humans and AI building together**. That means building applications powered by agents and designing the environments where agents help teams build better software.

I specialize in **Agent Security, Safety, Governance & Compliance**, treating controlled autonomy, accountability, and oversight as architectural requirements—not features to add after deployment.

Whether I’m writing **Rust**, designing an agent platform, or mentoring engineers, I stay close to the implementation.

## What I Do

### 🤖 AI Fleet Management & ADE Workflows

Design and orchestrate fleets of specialized agents within **Agentic Development Environments (ADEs)**. I connect planning, delegation, implementation, review, and verification into development workflows—with human judgment built into the process.

### 🧠 Agentic Applications & Applied AI

Architect and implement AI applications for **enterprises and startups**: tool-using agents, autonomous and semi-autonomous workflows, complex RAG pipelines, and human-in-the-loop systems.

My work spans model integration, context engineering, LoRA fine-tuning, and inference optimization.

### 🛡️ Agent Security, Safety, Governance & Compliance

Design agentic systems with explicit boundaries: what agents can access, which actions they can take, when approval is required, and how their behavior is reviewed.

I bring security, safety, governance, and compliance considerations into architecture and delivery—from controlled execution to accountability and auditability.

### ☁️ AI Platforms & Infrastructure

Connect agent applications with the infrastructure behind them: execution environments, tool integrations, model serving, retrieval, evaluation, and observability.

My focus is operating these systems with clear controls over reliability, access, performance, and cost.

### 🏗️ Architecture & Scaling

Build and evolve applications as their users, workloads, and business requirements grow. My background spans **distributed systems, microservices, event-driven architecture, cloud infrastructure, and end-to-end product delivery**.

### 🌱 Technical Leadership, Mentorship & Training

Lead as a **player-coach**: write code, guide architectural decisions, mentor developers, and deliver practical training.

I connect business goals with technical execution while helping teams strengthen their engineering practices and adopt AI-assisted development.
