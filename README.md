## What I Do

### 🤖 AI Fleet Management & ADE Workflows

Design and orchestrate fleets of specialized AI agents within **Agentic Development Environments (ADEs)**. I build workflows that automate and accelerate the software development lifecycle, combining agent execution with human oversight and engineering judgment.

### 🧠 Agentic Applications & Applied AI

Architect and implement production-grade AI applications for **enterprises and startups**—from autonomous and semi-autonomous agents to complex RAG pipelines and human-in-the-loop systems. My work spans model integration, LoRA fine-tuning, and inference optimization.

### 🛡️ Agent Security, Safety, Governance & Compliance

Specialize in securing and governing agentic systems. I design for controlled autonomy, clear accountability, auditability, and human oversight—bringing security, safety, and compliance considerations into the architecture from the start.

### 🏗️ Architecture & Scaling

Build and evolve applications as their users, workloads, and business requirements grow. My experience spans **distributed systems, microservices, event-driven architecture, and multi-cloud infrastructure**, with hands-on ownership across delivery and production operations.

### 🌱 Technical Leadership, Mentorship & Training

Lead as a **player-coach**: write code, guide architectural decisions, mentor developers, and deliver practical training. I connect business goals with technical execution while helping teams strengthen their engineering skills and adopt AI-assisted development.
